## Conflict decisions

- Billing: `CostInput` keeps upstream `DisableModelLongContextPricing` and the
  fork's `referenceModelCost` / `suppressHiddenConsumption`; the fallback path
  keeps `applyOpenAIConfiguredModelPrice`. In-flight reservation keeps the
  fork's estimator-side wallet-unit cap.
- **GPT-6.1 Sol subscription long-context exemption.** Adopted as upstream
  (owner decision 2026-10-13): OAuth/setup-token GPT-6.1 Sol requests no longer
  pay the model long-context surcharge in customer billing or account cost
  statistics; API-key accounts keep it. The fork hidden consumption multiplier
  and group rate still apply, so >272k OAuth Sol requests cost roughly half of
  the previous charge and no longer jump at the 272k boundary. The first
  release (`f9eeaf7eb`) briefly kept the customer surcharge behind a flag; that
  flag was removed. Upstream tests fold in the hidden multiplier.
- Gemini v1beta / cached-content billing errors use `googleBillingError` so the
  fork's business codes keep the `billing_error` type.
- `upstream_models.go` adopts upstream `readUpstreamModelsResponse` but resolves
  the proxy through `ResolveAccountProxyURL` (fixed egress), failing closed.
- Payment countdown keeps the fork's server-confirmed expiry; the panel and
  page show `paymentExpiryChecking` instead of upstream's local expiry.
- Reset-card use responses localize the group DTO.
- Ent and Wire were regenerated after the merge.
